DiscordNX2 verification

Temporary hosting

Hosting may stop at any time, without notice. No availability or uptime is guaranteed. This service is provided only until proper browser support is available on Switch 2.

Start from your Switch

Build and open DiscordNX2, then select Log in. If Discord requests CAPTCHA, scan the second QR with your phone camera. Keep both devices on the same Wi-Fi, complete the challenge yourself, and tap Send to Switch.

The QR supplies a short-lived verification session. Opening the verification page without a fresh QR cannot sign you in.

What this page does

It loads hCaptcha's official widget and encrypts the completed result for a direct connection to your Switch. It never receives your Discord login ticket, account token or private key. There is no account-token relay.

GitHub hosts these static files, and hCaptcha loads its own resources. A completed CAPTCHA does not guarantee that Discord will accept the login.

If hosting stops, CAPTCHA-required logins using this address may stop working. You may self-host the verification page and configure a different HTTPS address when building the NRO.
